## About

Dalron J. Robertson is a biomedical researcher, software engineer, PharmD/MBA candidate, and founder of NaS. His work focuses on precision medicine, translational science, clinical pharmacy, and computational approaches to biomedical research.

His background includes biological science, health-system pharmacy, software engineering, molecular modeling, bioinformatics, and business. He is developing a career grounded in direct patient care while building scientific systems that support better therapeutic decisions.

Oncology is the initial clinical and scientific focus. The longer-term vision extends this work into infectious disease and other areas where biomarkers, real-world evidence, and biomedical data can improve patient selection, treatment strategy, dosing, and therapeutic response.

## Current Focus
